Frequently Asked Questions

Find answers to common questions about using Council spaces.

▸ How far in advance can I make a reservation?
Bookings open up to 6 months in advance. Minimum booking notice is 48 business hours.
▸ Do I need insurance to book a venue?
Commercial users and public events require Public Liability Insurance (minimum $20M). Private events usually do not.
▸ What is the cancellation policy?
Full refunds are provided for cancellations made at least 7 days prior to the booking date.
